Clinical reference: blood marker ranges, nutrient reference values (NRVs) and plan rules, for the dietitians to decide. The database is live (migration 0051) and loaded from the two workbooks the practice prepared. Do NOT create migrations. Do NOT edit src/lib/engine.ts, src/lib/adjust.ts or src/lib/energy.ts. Show database refusals word for word.

OWNER'S DECISION
- Nothing here is used by the app until a dietitian decides it. These values will change over time.
- The reference values come from the workbooks and can't be edited in the app. Dietitians record the practice's own decision beside them.
- Admins and dietitians decide. Interns can read and comment.

DATABASE (live)
Tables with one row per workbook row:
- clinical_markers: 49 blood markers (Marker decisions tab)
- marker_ranges: 311 ranges by age, sex and pregnancy (Ranges by age tab)
- nutrient_reference_values: 310 NRV rows (NRVs tab)
- nutrition_targets: 11 (Targets and ranges tab)
- plan_rules: 9 (Plan rules tab)
- reference_decisions: 12 open questions D1–D12 (Decisions tab)
- reference_sources: references with links

Every row has:
- row_key
- ref (jsonb: the workbook's reference columns exactly as printed; show them as label: value)
- review_state:
  - 'proposed': not decided yet
  - 'decided'
  - 'changed': the workbook changed this row after it was decided; it's inactive until decided again. previous_ref holds the old values; show old → new.
- decided_by_name, decided_at (stamped by the database; show "Decided by … on …")
- comments
- retired_at (no longer in the workbook; hide by default)

Parsed fields on top of ref:
- marker_ranges:
  - marker_code, population, sex ('any' | 'male' | 'female'), age_from_days, age_to_days, pregnancy, unit, range_type
  - low, high: numbers read from the printed value; null when it isn't a plain number or a simple limit
  - low_text, high_text: as printed
  - is_gap: pink in the workbook, no Australian source
  - is_comparator: grey, New Zealand comparison only
- nutrient_reference_values: nutrient_code, nutrient, unit, food_column (null = not stored in the food library yet), life_stage, sex, status ('general' | 'pregnancy' | 'lactation'), ear, rdi, ai, ul, sdt, ul_applies_to
- nutrition_targets: code, what, unit, life_stage, sex, lower, upper
- plan_rules: code, rule
- reference_decisions: code, question

The decision fields the app writes (update by row_key; send them together):
- clinical_markers: track (boolean), practice_range, refer_when, food_action, retest_window, variation_pct, practice_note
- marker_ranges: decision ('agree' | 'reject' | 'edit'), practice_low, practice_high, dietitian_note. 'edit' needs practice_low or practice_high (the database refuses otherwise).
- nutrient_reference_values: decision, check_against ('ear' | 'rdi' | 'ai' | 'sdt'), practice_target, practice_ceiling, use_in_checks (boolean)
- nutrition_targets: decision, practice_lower, practice_upper, use_in_checks
- plan_rules: use_rule (boolean), practice_defaults, when_to_offer, practice_note
- reference_decisions: decision (text), settings (text)
- every table: comments (any staff), change_reason (the "why", optional; it goes into the change log)

The database refuses:
- changing ref or any parsed field;
- inserting or deleting rows;
- interns changing anything but comments.

It stamps who and when, and logs every change in reference_change_log (at, by_name, table_name, row_key, field, old_value, new_value, reason, kind).

Views:
- reference_review_progress (tab, total, decided, changed)
- active_marker_ranges, active_nutrient_reference_values, active_nutrition_targets, active_plan_rules: decided rows only. Nothing uses these yet; the planner and the results reader will.
