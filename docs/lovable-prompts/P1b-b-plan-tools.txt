1. Adjust one meal (item ingredient changes).
   - On a recipe item in the plan editor, "Adjust this meal" opens the existing AdjustPanel for that item's recipe, starting from its variation's lines (resolveVersion).
   - Saving does NOT create a recipe profile. It writes meal_plan_item_deltas for that item:
     - Diff the variation's lines against the adjusted lines with diffToDeltas.
     - Insert each change with item_id and source 'adjust', and with base_section set to the changed line's section (the section an added line goes into).
     - Replace any earlier ones: delete where item_id = this item, then insert.
   - Only lines with a real ingredient line id (line_ref) can be changed or removed. Lines a variation added stay as they are.
   - Quantities are whole-recipe, as in profiles.
   - The item row shows "Adjusted for this plan" with a Reset button (deletes its deltas).
   - The recipe and its variations are never changed.
2. Cook extra (leftovers).
   - On a recipe item, "Cook extra for…" picks a later day and slot (and serves, default 1).
   - It makes the original item batch_role 'cook' and inserts a new item with:
     - the same recipe and variation
     - the chosen day and slot
     - the serves picked
     - batch_role 'eat'
     - the same new batch_group (a fresh uuid shared by both)
   - The cook item shows "Cook N serves: X tonight, Y for Wednesday lunch".
   - The leftover shows "Leftovers from Monday dinner".
   - Removing the cook item: ask first. Then remove its leftovers too, or turn them into normal meals (clear batch_group and batch_role) if the dietitian prefers.
   - A leftover can't move before its cook day; the database's publish check also refuses it.
   - Shopping already counts every serve, so nothing changes there.
3. Review and confirm. Show this panel only when meal_plans.report is set (the generator, coming later, sets it).
   - It shows the report.
   - A notes box (max 2,000 characters) and Confirm, which calls confirm_plan(p_plan_id, p_notes).
   - When confirmed, show "Confirmed by {confirmed_by_name} on {date}" with the notes, and an Undo button: confirm_plan(p_plan_id, null, false).
   - Any change to the plan's food, days or dates clears the confirmation in the database. Reload after edits so the panel shows that.
   - Any staff member who can open the plan can confirm (owner decision). Publish shows the database's blocker if the plan isn't confirmed.
   - Published versions keep the notes and confirmer (meal_plan_versions.review_notes, confirmed_by_name). Show them in the version history.
4. History.
   - After each successful change in the plan editor, insert one plan_edits row (meal_plan_id, operation, diff), fire-and-forget. The database stamps who and when.

   - diff is a small JSON object of what changed (ids and before/after values).
   - A "History" drawer on the plan lists them, newest first: who, when, and a plain sentence ("Changed serves of Thai beef salad on Tuesday lunch from 1 to 1.25").
