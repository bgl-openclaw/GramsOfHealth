Add to project knowledge (mem://foods.md, create it if missing): "A food has three states: Verified (foods.verified, a dietitian checked it), AI verified (foods.ai_verified and not verified: numbers come from AUSNUT 2023 with evidence, not yet checked by a dietitian), Draft (neither). Dietitian verification always wins. Client screens only ever use dietitian verification."

WHAT CHANGED IN THE DATABASE
- foods gains: ausnut_key (text, e.g. "F006081"), ai_verified (boolean, default false), ai_verified_at (timestamptz), ai_match_confidence ('exact_key' | 'agreed_list' | 'high_match' | 'checked_verified'), ai_evidence (one line, max 400 characters). Screens that load foods with select * get these automatically.
- The database clears AI verification by itself when someone changes kcal, protein, fat, carbs, fibre or density, changes a tag, or marks the food unverified. The app never needs to manage that.
- recipe_verification gains three columns at the end: ai_verified_food_count, ai_verified_food_names (text[]), food_note (e.g. "3 foods AI verified, 1 unverified"). `status` and `reasons` are unchanged: a recipe whose foods are all verified OR AI verified, and is otherwise complete, is status 'ai_verified'.
- food_leverage gains ai_verified and awaiting_dietitian_recipes. blocked_recipes now treats AI verified foods as done.
- changes_to_review gains food_ai_verified_now. Import changes carry a note at the end of the summary, e.g. "[AUSNUT 2023 F006316]".
