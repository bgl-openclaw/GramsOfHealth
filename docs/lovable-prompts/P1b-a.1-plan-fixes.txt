APP FIXES
1. Item changes keep their saved section. In plan-items.ts (resolvePlanItem), use the row's own base_section and only look it up when it's null:
   base_section: d.base_section ?? resolved.lines.find(l => l.id === d.line_ref)?.section ?? null
   Keep base_section in the PlanItemDelta type. Today an added ingredient always lands in the unnamed section, and a line whose id has gone can't be matched if it had a section.
2. Never publish a partial snapshot.
   - buildPlanSnapshot skips items it can't resolve (`if (it.recipe_id && !full) continue;` and `if (resolved.unavailable) continue;`). It must instead throw an error naming the item, its day and its slot, e.g. "Thai beef salad (Tuesday lunch) can't be worked out. Fix or remove it before publishing."
   - In onPublish, re-run the unavailable check on the freshly loaded items and recipes, not the page's older state.
   - Today the database refuses with "The plan changed while publishing", which sends people looking in the wrong place.
3. Problem alternatives must be visible.
   - In the Swap list, show an alternative's status when it can't be worked out ("Needs fixing"), with its remove button.
   - The publish blocker message names the items, marking alternatives as "(alternative)".
   - The shopping PDF button and the draft print show that message instead of doing nothing or failing.
4. Load item changes through the plan, not a list of ids:
   select("*, meal_plan_items!inner(meal_plan_id)").eq("meal_plan_items.meal_plan_id", planId), then drop the embedded field.
   A list of ids in the URL breaks on large plans (the planner will make 28-day plans with alternatives).
5. An alternative uses the same serves as the meal it replaces.
   - When adding one, set its servings_override to the serves the main meal shows, not the main's raw servings_override (often null).
   - After a swap, the meal should then match the kcal and protein the Swap list showed.
6. The client's own free-choice slots (from their meal structure) work as before: Add, Add food and dropping still work. Only a saved slot state (free choice or empty for that day, from meal_plan_slot_states) hides them.
7. Inputs match the database rules, so people get a clear message instead of a raw error:
   - "% of the day": min 1, max 100 (the database requires more than 0).
   - Slot reason: maxLength 300.
8. Publish dialog:
   - Clear the "Client sees it from" date after a successful publish.
   - The date input's minimum is today (Sydney date).
9. Undo after deleting a recipe brings back everything that was removed with its plan items: their top-ups, alternatives and item changes, not just the items.
10. The collapsed-sidebar dot (Changes and My profile) goes back to the same orange as the other menu counts. bg-accent is pale green and vanishes on the highlighted row.
