BACKEND CONTRACT
- Every new food, food edit/delete, unit-conversion change (incl. default sizes) and food tag change is logged automatically by the database in change_log. Readable by clinicians (admin, dietitian) only.
- View changes_to_review (unreviewed only): id, entity ('food' | 'unit_conversion' | 'food_tag'), food_id, food_name, action ('insert' | 'update' | 'delete'), summary (plain words — show verbatim), auto_unverified (boolean), changed_at, changed_by, changed_by_name ('System / import' when no person), food_verified_now (null if the food was deleted), changes (jsonb detail).
- RPC mark_changes_reviewed({ p_ids: number[] | null }) → number marked. null = mark everything currently unreviewed. Clinicians only.
- Automatic rule: if someone changes a VERIFIED food's kcal/protein/fat/carbs/fibre per 100 g, its density, or its allergen/diet tags, the database marks it UNVERIFIED (never the other way). Its recipes drop to Needs review until a dietitian re-verifies the food.
